# Upcoming actions

Overview pairs **Needs attention** with **Next 30 days** below the net-worth
and available-funds cards. Data-health issues retain their severity, and
investment reviews retain their NAV condition. Current valuation completeness,
liquidity completeness and historical data health are separate statuses.
The next-30-days card previews up to three attention or scheduled items;
**View all** opens both bands when there are items. Empty results use a short
message without a zero-count link; eligible items and data checks keep a
separate review link. Monitoring setup, paused reminders, insufficient NAV data
and enabled monitoring without pending reminders are distinct states. Each
report retains its own source read time and timezone.

A past blocked product belongs to attention. The total counts each item once.
Eligible items and data checks are shown separately, so they do not inflate
the two main bands.
Each band initially shows up to 50 items. **Show 50 more** expands both bands,
and **Schedules** opens the scheduled items directly.

One product lifecycle appears as one item even if maturity, eligibility and
estimated receipt have different dates. Maturity is a contract date;
eligibility is when normal access can be requested; estimated receipt is a
projection under the recorded rules. None of these dates proves cash arrived.
Unknown proceeds stay unknown, and amounts from different currencies are never
added together in this view. Only normal access routes are included.

**Verify receipt** opens the product's receipt draft after a fresh state read
and only while receipt confirmation remains permitted. Review actual principal,
interest and fees, then preview and confirm if cash has arrived. Opening or
canceling the draft does not settle a product. A successful settlement removes
its upcoming item; undo restores it. Recording interest alone does not settle it.

**View terms** opens the product or source's current access rules. **Check
reservations** opens the relevant reservation list. When one item has both a
rules issue and a reservation issue, both links appear. These are navigation
shortcuts, not automatic financial actions. Refresh after changing dates, terms
or reservations to see the current projection. A stale target shows a message
instead of applying an old action.
