# v0.1.4 Release Contract

## Purpose and Status

**Release theme:** Cost Basis and Gain — know what a position cost and what it
is worth now.

**Status:** `Direction confirmed; Phases 0–9 implemented; public distribution checks pending`.

v0.1.4 adds an average cost basis to investment Holdings and derives
realized/unrealized gain and a simple currency decomposition from it. It
deliberately does not add tax-lot (FIFO) accounting, time-weighted or
money-weighted return, benchmarks, or a full net-worth attribution bridge —
those remain open for a later release. This document owns product scope and
acceptance. The [compatibility baseline](v0.1.4-baseline.md) records the
verified v0.1.3 dependency. The
[technical design](v0.1.4-technical-design.md) owns domain, schema, and
service contracts. The [implementation plan](v0.1.4-implementation-plan.md)
owns delivery order.

The archived Rust/Tauri v0.1.4 documents were reviewed as design input only.
Their FIFO lot ledger, unknown-basis declaration workflow, TWR/XIRR return
engine, and attribution bridge are explicitly **not** adopted by this
contract; see [Excluded](#excluded).

## Product Precondition

Nestworth-go has not published a public release. v0.1.4 therefore does not
carry a burden of reconstructing cost for positions a real user already
holds without a recorded price: the product can simply require a cost and
date at the moment a position first enters recorded history. See
[baseline](v0.1.4-baseline.md#product-precondition-no-public-release-exists).

## Release Goal

For any investment Holding, a Household can answer:

1. What did this position cost, on average, per unit?
2. How much have we gained or lost on it — realized and unrealized?
3. Of that gain or loss, how much came from the Instrument's own price
   movement, and how much came from currency movement?

The central invariants are:

> Every Holding that has ever held a positive quantity inside recorded history
> has a resolvable average cost. There is no "unknown basis" state in v0.1.4.

> Cost basis and gain are derived from the same immutable Activity evidence
> already recorded in v0.1.3. Recording a cost never fabricates a Buy, Sell,
> or Transfer, and never changes an existing Activity, effect, or Starting
> Point component.

> Missing input (for example, a missing current quote) keeps the cost basis
> visible and marks the gain unavailable. It is never displayed as zero.

## User Stories

- As a user starting history with an existing Instrument position, I record
  its per-unit cost and acquisition date once, alongside the quantity I
  already confirm today.
- As an investor, when I buy more of something I already hold, I do not enter
  a separate cost — the trade I already record is the cost, and Nestworth
  blends it into the position's average automatically.
- As a user reconciling a Holding that already existed outside a trade (the
  existing "already existed" workflow), I additionally record its per-unit
  cost, pre-filled to today's quote so I am not forced to research a real
  historical price if I do not know or care about it.
- As an investor, I can see each Holding's average cost, total cost, realized
  gain, and unrealized gain in my Household's base currency.
- As an investor, I can see how much of a Holding's gain came from the
  Instrument's price versus currency movement.
- As an investor, I can select 30 days, one year, or all time and see how much
  gain I realized by selling within that window.
- As an offline user, every v0.1.4 view remains available and correct without
  a provider request; a missing quote explains itself instead of hiding or
  zeroing a number.

## Included

### Cost Basis Capture

Average cost method only; there is no per-lot (FIFO) ledger.

| Entry point | What is captured | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting Point, for a Holding with positive quantity | Per-unit cost and acquisition date | Current quote price and today's date; user may override before confirming |
| First Buy for a brand-new Holding | Nothing new — the Buy's Quantity, gross total, and effective time already are the cost | Not applicable; the trade is real |
| "Already existed" reconciliation (`PositionAdjustmentInput`, adding quantity) | Per-unit cost | Current quote price and today's date; user may override |
| Any later Buy of an existing Holding | Nothing new — the trade's own gross total and effective time are used | Not applicable |

A Holding's average cost is the quantity-weighted blend of every non-reversed
cost-bearing event affecting it, in order: its Starting Point cost (if any),
every Buy, every Position Transfer received (blended in at the sending
Holding's average cost as of the transfer), each reducing event leaving the
average cost of the remaining quantity unchanged. See the
[technical design](v0.1.4-technical-design.md) for the exact replay contract.

### Realized and Unrealized Gain

- **Unrealized gain** for a Holding: current value at the selected/current
  quote, minus the quantity's average cost, in the Instrument's quote
  currency and in the Household base currency.
- **Realized gain** for a Sell: the sale's derived unit price minus the
  average cost in effect immediately before that sale, times the quantity
  sold, minus the sale's allocated explicit fee. A trade's explicit fee
  reduces realized gain; it is never capitalized into cost.
- Gain is reported at Instrument and Account scope. Portfolio- and
  Household-level aggregation is deferred.
- A missing current quote keeps cost basis and any already-realized gain
  visible; it marks unrealized gain unavailable rather than zero or stale.

### Currency Decomposition

For a Holding's unrealized gain in base currency, v0.1.4 shows two
components that sum exactly to the total:

- **Instrument movement** — the gain attributable to the Instrument's own
  price change, valued at the acquisition-time exchange rate.
- **Currency movement** — the remainder, attributable to exchange-rate
  movement between acquisition and now.

An Instrument quoted in the Household's base currency has zero currency
movement by construction and needs no FX quote. A missing FX quote at either
endpoint marks the decomposition unavailable for that Holding without
blocking its cost basis or its native-currency gain.

### Period Selection

- History's existing 30-day/one-year/all-time range selector, reused for
  v0.1.4, scopes **realized gain**: the sum of realized gain from Sells whose
  effective date falls inside the selected range, at Instrument and Account
  scope.
- Cost basis and unrealized gain are always "as of now" values; they are not
  period-scoped, because an average-cost position's gain since acquisition is
  the meaningful number, not a windowed rate of return.

### Presentation

- The Investments page gains an average cost, total cost, and unrealized
  gain column per position.
- The Analytics page gains a realized-gain-by-period view and a currency
  decomposition view, both at Instrument and Account scope, each with an
  accessible table.
- The Starting Point and "already existed" forms gain one additional cost
  field, pre-filled and explained in plain language — never presented as a
  ledger or tax concept.

## Excluded

v0.1.4 does not implement:

- FIFO or any per-lot cost basis; average cost is the only method;
- an "unknown basis" state or a declare/revoke workflow for it;
- TWR, XIRR, money-weighted return, or any annualized/rate-of-return claim;
- benchmarks or any comparison to a market index;
- a full Household net-worth attribution bridge (savings contribution, fees,
  and an explicit unexplained residual);
- Portfolio- or Household-level gain/decomposition aggregation;
- gain, cost basis, or currency decomposition for non-investment (manually
  valued) Accounts;
- gain or decomposition attached to a single Timeline entry; the period
  selector is the only windowed view;
- income/dividend attribution, tax reporting, or tax-lot optimization;
- any change to how v0.1.3 Activities, effects, or Starting Point components
  are recorded, classified, or replayed for non-cost-basis purposes.

## Locked Product Decisions

| Decision | v0.1.4 contract |
| --- | --- |
| Cost method | Average cost per Holding; no FIFO lot ledger |
| Unknown basis | Does not exist; every positive-quantity Holding has a resolvable cost |
| Cost capture timing | Only at Starting Point capture and "already existed" reconciliation; ordinary Buys need no extra input |
| Cost default | Current quote price and today's date; always user-overridable before saving |
| Average cost on removal | Unchanged by Sell, Transfer-out, or a downward `PositionAdjustmentInput`; only the remaining quantity's basis carries forward |
| Average cost on Transfer-in | Blended from the sending Holding's average cost as of the transfer |
| Realized gain | Computed at the moment of replay using the average cost in effect immediately before the Sell; reduced by the sale's explicit fee |
| Undo/Fix | Recomputes cost basis and gain as if the undone/corrected Activity never existed, by excluding reversed pairs from replay |
| Currency decomposition | Exactly two components (Instrument movement, currency movement) that sum to the total; no unexplained residual concept in v0.1.4 |
| Missing input | Keep cost basis and realized gain visible; mark unrealized gain/decomposition unavailable, never zero or substituted |
| Scope | Instrument and Account only; Portfolio and Household aggregation deferred |
| Financial authority | Go services derive cost, gain, and decomposition; Fyne formats and lays out results only |

## Acceptance Scenarios

### Starting Point With an Existing Position

A Household confirms **Start history here** with an existing `10 QQQ`
position. The Starting Point form additionally asks for QQQ's per-unit cost,
defaulting to the current quote (say `100 USD`) and today's date. The user
overrides it to `80 USD` because they remember buying lower. Average cost is
recorded as `80 USD`; no Activity is created.

### Buying More of an Existing Position

The Household later buys `5` more QQQ for a gross total of `600 USD`
(`120 USD` each). Average cost becomes:

```text
(10 × 80 + 5 × 120) / 15 = 1400 / 15 = 93.33 USD
```

No separate cost field appears on this Buy form; the trade itself is the cost
input.

### Selling Part of a Position

The Household sells `6` QQQ at `150 USD` each (gross `900 USD`, no fee).
Average cost immediately before the sale is `93.33 USD`. Realized gain is:

```text
6 × (150 - 93.33) = 6 × 56.67 = 340.02 USD
```

The remaining `9` QQQ keep an average cost of `93.33 USD`; it does not change
because units were removed, not added.

### Undoing a Buy

The Household undoes the `5`-share Buy from the second scenario. Average cost
returns to exactly `80 USD` for the remaining quantity, and the later Sell's
realized gain recomputes using `80 USD` as the pre-sale average cost, as if
the undone Buy had never happened.

### Currency Decomposition

A Household holds a USD Instrument bought at `80 USD` when `1 USD = 6.8 CNY`,
now quoted at `100 USD` when `1 USD = 7.1 CNY`, base currency CNY, quantity
`10`. Total unrealized gain in CNY:

```text
Cost (CNY)    = 10 × 80 × 6.8   = 5440
Value (CNY)   = 10 × 100 × 7.1  = 7100
Gain (CNY)    = 7100 - 5440     = 1660
Instrument movement = 10 × (100 - 80) × 6.8 = 1360
Currency movement   = 1660 - 1360           = 300
```

Both components are shown together; neither is presented alone as "the"
gain.

### Missing Current Quote

A Holding has an average cost of `80 USD` but no current quote is selected.
Cost basis and total quantity remain visible. Unrealized gain and currency
decomposition show **Unavailable** with a direct action to add a dated
manual quote, consistent with existing v0.1.2/v0.1.3 completeness language.

## Release Acceptance

v0.1.4 is development-complete only when:

- Phase 0 freezes the actual completed v0.1.3 schema-5 fixture, service
  interfaces, and Fyne page ownership before schema-6 work starts.
- Migration `5 -> 6` preserves every v0.1.1/v0.1.2/v0.1.3 identity,
  observation, selected current value, and future-schema zero-write/no-network
  guarantee.
- Starting Point capture for a positive-quantity Holding requires and stores a
  per-unit cost and date; it commits zero Activities, matching v0.1.3.
- `PositionAdjustmentInput` with `Added=true` requires a per-unit cost when it
  is establishing a Holding's first positive quantity outside a trade.
- Average cost, realized gain, and unrealized gain match the release
  acceptance examples exactly, including after Undo.
- Currency decomposition sums exactly to the total gain for every fixture
  case with complete inputs, and reports unavailable rather than a partial or
  substituted number when an input is missing.
- The period selector scopes realized gain only; cost basis and unrealized
  gain remain "as of now" values in every view.
- No v0.1.4 read path performs a network request or an unbounded query over a
  Holding's full Activity history in a way that violates the query-bound
  principle already applied to historical valuation.
- Fyne performs no cost, gain, or decomposition arithmetic; it only requests
  and formats Go results.
- Domain, migration, replay, gain, currency-decomposition, query-bound, UI,
  locale, race, golden, and compatibility tests pass through the normal Go
  repository gates.
- The Phase 9 arm64 packaging smoke produces an unsigned `.app` and UDZO DMG;
  manual accessibility review, signing, notarization, and artifact retention
  remain public-distribution gates. Before any public release that can
  encounter a real pre-v0.1.4 database, the schema `5 -> 6` development-fixture
  backfill policy must be revisited.

## Compatibility Promises

v0.1.4 preserves every active v0.1.1, v0.1.2, and v0.1.3 promise and adds:

- Average cost and gain are read models derived from existing Activity and
  Starting Point evidence; they introduce no new interpretation of an
  existing Activity, effect, or Starting Point component.
- A later release may add per-lot (FIFO) tracking, return calculation, or a
  full attribution bridge, but may not reinterpret a v0.1.4 average-cost
  result as if it had always been lot-based.
- The Starting Point cost and every recorded trade's cost inputs remain
  immutable evidence once saved; only Undo/Fix, using the existing v0.1.3
  correction mechanism, changes what is included in a later replay.
